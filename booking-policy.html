Booking policy

Holds, cancellations, and refunds for paid sessions.

Holding a place

When you continue to payment we hold the place for 40 minutes. If you don’t pay, it goes back on sale.

You cancel

Cancel online (email link or your account) 24 hours or more before the session starts. The place goes back on sale and Stripe refunds you. Inside 24 hours, email the club — we decide case by case.

We cancel

If the club cancels the session, everyone who paid is refunded and emailed.

Tickets

Bring the QR from your confirmation email or account. One code per attendee.

Safeguarding

We need date of birth, emergency contact, and photo/video consent for every attendee. Under-18s must be booked by a parent or guardian.
