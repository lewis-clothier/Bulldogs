Privacy

What we keep, why, and how to ask us to change it. Review with an adviser if you need a formal policy.

We store booking, order, and account details so we can run sessions and send merch. That includes names, emails, phone numbers, emergency contacts, dates of birth, medical notes you give us, and delivery addresses.

Why

To take payment (via Stripe), confirm places, check people in, email receipts, and — only if you tick the box — send newsletters. Coaches see safeguarding fields on the day.

Who else

Stripe (payments), Supabase (database), Resend (email), and Tapstitch when we place a merch order. We don’t sell your data.

Children

A parent or guardian should book anyone under 18. We keep date of birth and emergency contacts for session safety.

Your choices

Email bulldogs.limited@gmail.com to stop the newsletter, or to ask what we hold and to correct or delete it where the law allows.
