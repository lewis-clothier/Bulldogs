Privacy notice

What we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and your rights. Last updated 21 November 2026.

Who we are

Team Bulldogs is run by Team Bulldogs Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17350846), of 19 Hunstanton Drive, Binfield, Bracknell, England, RG42 4JA. We are the “controller” of the personal information on this site. Contact us about anything on this page at bulldogs.limited@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Booking details: the booker’s name, email and phone number, so we can take the booking, send tickets and contact you about the session. Legal basis: to carry out our agreement with you (contract).
  • Attendee details: each attendee’s name and date of birth, to check age groups and run the session safely. Legal basis: contract and our legitimate interest in running safe sessions.
  • Emergency contact: a name and phone number for each attendee, used only if something happens at a session. Legal basis: legitimate interests and, in an emergency, protecting someone’s vital interests.
  • Medical and allergy notes (optional): anything you choose to tell coaches. This is health information, so we only keep it with your explicit consent, which you give at booking. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us and we’ll delete the notes.
  • Photos and video (optional): we only use photos or video of an attendee if you ticked the box for them. You can withdraw this at any time; we’ll stop using new material and remove what we reasonably can.
  • Shop orders: name, email, phone and delivery address, to make and deliver your order. Legal basis: contract.
  • Accounts (optional): your email, name and a password (stored scrambled, never readable by us), or your Google sign-in, so you can see your bookings and orders. Legal basis: contract.
  • Event and merch emails (optional): your email address, only after you confirm the sign-up from your inbox. Legal basis: consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Payments: Stripe handles card details. We never see or store your card number, only whether a payment succeeded or was refunded.

We also keep financial records of payments and refunds because the law requires it (legal obligation).

Children

Bookings and accounts must be made by an adult. A parent or guardian must make the booking for anyone under 18 and confirms this when booking. We use a child’s details only to run the session safely.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising. We use these services to run the site, and they only use your information on our instructions:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in system, hosted in Ireland (EU).
  • Stripe: card payments and refunds.
  • Resend: sends our emails (based in the USA).
  • Royal Mail or another UK courier: delivers merch orders, so it receives your name and delivery address. We print merch in bulk and post orders ourselves, so our printer never receives your details.
  • GitHub: hosts the website's pages, so it sees visitors' IP addresses (based in the USA).
  • Google: provides the fonts on this site and the optional “Sign in with Google”. Loading the fonts sends your IP address to Google.

Where a service stores information outside the UK, we rely on the protections UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK’s approved contract terms.

Club coaches and organisers see attendee details for the sessions they run. Only signed-in club admins can see booking and order information.

How long we keep it

  • Medical notes and emergency contacts: deleted automatically 30 days after the session.
  • Bookings and orders: deleted automatically 7 years after the event or order, because we must keep financial records for 6 years after the end of each financial year.
  • Waiting-list sign-ups: deleted 30 days after the event.
  • Unpaid or abandoned bookings: attendee dates of birth are deleted after 30 days.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account (from the account menu) or ask us to.
  • Email sign-ups: until you unsubscribe. We keep a note of unsubscribed addresses so we don’t email them again.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, to object to how we use it, or to send it to you in a reusable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email bulldogs.limited@gmail.com and we’ll reply within one month. You can delete your account yourself from the account menu.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and browser storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores only what the site needs to work: keeping you signed in and remembering your bag. These are strictly necessary, so we don’t ask for permission to use them.

Keeping it safe

Booking and order information can only be read by the club’s systems and signed-in club admins. Connections to the site are encrypted.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
