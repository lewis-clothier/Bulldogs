Delivery & returns

Print-on-demand tees via Tapstitch. Not next-day.

Made to order

We place your order with our printer after you pay. Typical dispatch is around 2–3 weeks. We’ll email when it’s in.

Delivery

UK only for now. Delivery is added at Stripe checkout. You give the shipping address there.

After you pay

You can’t cancel merch online once paid — we may already have sent it to print. Email the club quickly if something’s wrong. If we haven’t marked the order fulfilled we can often refund from our side.

Returns

Faulty or mis-printed items: email photos and we’ll sort a reprint or refund. Change-of-mind returns may not apply to personalised/print-to-order kit — we’ll say so on the product if that changes. Have a solicitor check this if you need a hard consumer-law version.
