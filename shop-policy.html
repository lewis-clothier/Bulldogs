Delivery & returns

Club tees, packed and posted by us. Not next-day.

Dispatch

In-stock sizes are posted from the club’s own stock. If your size isn’t in stock, we order a new print run first, which typically takes 2–3 weeks. We’ll email you when your order is posted.

Delivery

UK only for now. Delivery is added at Stripe checkout. You give the shipping address there.

Changing your mind

You can cancel your order for any reason from the moment you pay until 14 days after it arrives. Email bulldogs.limited@gmail.com with your order reference to tell us.

  • If it hasn’t been dispatched yet, we’ll cancel it and refund you in full.
  • If it has arrived, send it back unworn and in its original condition within 14 days of telling us. We’ll refund the item price and the standard delivery charge within 14 days of getting it back (or of you showing proof that you posted it).
  • You pay the cost of posting a change-of-mind return.

This doesn’t apply to items made specially for you, for example with a name or number you chose. We’ll make that clear on the product.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, mis-printed or isn’t what you ordered, email us photos within 30 days and we’ll send a replacement or a full refund, including return postage. This doesn’t affect your legal rights.
