Why it works

  • Keeps today’s mood, but every section has one clear job.
  • Rails scale to any number of events or athletes without breaking layout.
  • “Next up” strip means the next booking is one tap from the banner.

Watch out for

  • Rails hide content off-screen — needs arrows and swipe hints.
  • Event posters want a photo each (can fall back to a brand graphic).

Site-wide

  • Events page = full rail + list toggle.
  • Shop = spotlight per product.
  • Modals and account menu use the same glass panels.
